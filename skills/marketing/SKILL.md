# Marketing skill

## Principles

- Lead with audience pain and a clear offer.
- Prefer specific claims over hype; flag unproven claims.
- Ship drafts the user can paste (email, landing, social, brief).

## Output

- One primary deliverable (brief, landing section, email, or calendar).
- Optional checklist for launch or distribution.
